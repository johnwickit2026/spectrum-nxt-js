# Spectrum International School: names and published roles

This roster covers named employees and role-holders shown on the school’s public website. Titles and name spellings are reproduced as published, with only light formatting for readability.

## Leadership and named governance roles

- **Nur Ahammed Khokan** — Founder & CEO. The Founder & CEO message is signed with this name. [1]
- **Nur Ahammed** — CEO & Founder, as listed on the Non-Academic Staff page. The site does not establish whether this is the same person as Nur Ahammed Khokan, so I have kept the two displayed names separate. [2]
- **Sharmin Akter Eva** — Vice Principal & Academic Head in the Academic Head message; listed as Vice-Principal in the non-academic staff directory. [2] [3]
- **Mufti Shamsuddin Zia** — Chairman, Shariah Supervisory Committee. [4]

The general committee page describes advisory, executive, Sharia, curriculum, and academic-stage structures but does not name committee members or assign named stage leaders. The Shariah roster also displays “Member Two” and “Member Three” without personal names. [4] [5]
